Focu — Source Library

Focu: the way to digest information in the agentic age.

Curated source packs for Focu. You choose the people and publications; Focu digests what they publish into one short daily briefing. Every pack states its attention cost up front, in posts/month, and installs only after a preview. No ranking, no engagement metrics, nothing installed silently.

The machine-readable catalog is catalog.json; the recommended default is default.json.
