# Research-first, multi-format delivery

## Principle

The **Evidence Pack is the immutable source of truth**. Its cited Markdown research report is the detailed reading experience, not a transcript. Podcasts are concise, listening-optimized derivatives. An Open Notebook transcript is a record of what was spoken and **may contain unsupported elaboration**; it must not replace the research report.

One research intent and Evidence Pack can have several presentation artifacts. They share `evidence_id`, not necessarily the same prose or level of detail:

| Artifact | Style | Provenance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `research_markdown` | Complete findings, uncertainty, explicit citations and original URLs | Deterministically rendered from stored Evidence Pack |
| `podcast_transcript` | Spoken narration/dialogue; may be compressed or embellished | Open Notebook's completed episode response |
| `podcast_outline` | Spoken-program structure | Open Notebook's completed episode response |
| `podcast_metadata` | Episode name, voice/profile snapshots, audio reference | Open Notebook's completed episode response |

The report generated by Focu contains **all claims present in the Evidence Pack**, not automatically all content of the original articles. If the user wants more detailed interpretation than the Evidence Pack contains, the host agent must perform an explicitly approved research or writing step, preserve its supporting sources, and validate any new claims before publication. Do not invent facts or promote an unverified podcast claim to canonical evidence.

## Muse acceptance flow

Use the existing `focu ingest-evidence` handoff, then:

```bash
.venv/bin/focu report ev_REAL_ID --output data/reports/research.md
.venv/bin/focu podcast generate --content-file data/reports/research.md \
  --episode-name "Daily research briefing" \
  --episode-profile "EXISTING_EPISODE_PROFILE" \
  --speaker-profile "EXISTING_SPEAKER_PROFILE"
.venv/bin/focu podcast job "JOB_ID"
.venv/bin/focu podcast episodes
.venv/bin/focu podcast download "EPISODE_ID" --output data/episodes/briefing.mp3
.venv/bin/focu sync-podcast --evidence-id ev_REAL_ID --episode-id "EPISODE_ID"
.venv/bin/focu artifacts ev_REAL_ID
.venv/bin/focu artifact "podcast_transcript:EPISODE_ID" --output data/episodes/transcript.json
```

Use actual IDs/profile names. Do not sync a queued or failed episode. Open Notebook may return transcript/outline as structured JSON rather than Markdown; Focu archives that **losslessly as JSON**. The Markdown report is the user-readable detailed version. MP3 bytes are stored on disk and served by Open Notebook, not duplicated into SQLite.

## Interaction-specific editorial policy

- **Audio:** 5–10 minute (configurable) synthesis, explain why a topic matters, natural spoken language, fewer figures; never invent conclusions or claims not present in evidence.
- **Reading:** fuller, navigable Markdown with all verified claims, caveats, primary-source URLs and source identifiers; don't sacrifice traceability for fluency.
- **Transcript:** preserve what was actually said, including potential mistakes; never silently rewrite it into a factual report.
- **Future delivery channels:** implement as additional renderers from the same evidence; don't mutate the canonical Evidence Pack.

## Known limitations

The archive is an additive SQLite table in the Focu DB. Sync is **explicit**, not triggered automatically when a podcast job completes. The generated Markdown is an evidence-based structured report, not a new long-form LLM essay. No backfill of existing Open Notebook episodes occurs without a known `evidence_id` and episode ID. Reusing an artifact ID with different content fails rather than silently overwriting it. A completed episode can still lack a transcript or outline; the archive records only the fields returned by the server. A source URL in a transcript does not prove its claim.
